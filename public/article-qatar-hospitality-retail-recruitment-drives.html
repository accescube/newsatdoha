Qatar Hospitality & Retail Sectors Announce Mega Walk-In Recruitment Drives in Doha

മലയാളത്തിൽ: ഖത്തറിലെ ഹോസ്പിറ്റാലിറ്റി, റീട്ടെയിൽ മേഖലകളിൽ വൻ തൊഴിലവസരങ്ങൾ; വാക്ക്-ഇൻ ഇന്റർവ്യൂകൾ ദോഹയിൽ
Careers Desk 10 Sep 2026, 12:30 PM 3 min read
Font Size:
Share:
Major retail conglomerates, premier luxury hotels, and logistics firms in Doha have scheduled direct walk-in interviews this week for over 450 vacancies across multiple designations.

Job seekers in Qatar with valid QID and NOC status have significant opportunities this week as leading hospitality chains, retail hypermarkets, and supply chain operators launch open recruitment drives.

Open Positions Include:

  • Front Desk Executives & Guest Relations Officers
  • F&B Service Crew, Baristas & Continental Chefs
  • Retail Cashiers, Inventory Supervisors & Merchandisers
  • Light and Heavy Drivers with valid Qatar Driving License
  • Digital Marketing Specialists & IT Support Technicians

Candidates are advised to bring updated CVs, passport copies, valid QID, and educational credentials. The interviews are scheduled at designated corporate headquarters in West Bay and Ain Khaled.
